Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Scribec ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use the Scribec Android application. We are committed to transparency and securing your information.

2. Data Collection & Processing

Scribec captures your screen and audio to provide seamless recording and transcription features. Screen recordings and video files are stored entirely locally on your device. Our speech-to-text transcription feature requires sending audio data to Groq API for processing. Once transcribed, the resulting text is stored locally on your device.

3. Permissions We Request

  • Microphone (Required): Captures audio for screen recordings and live transcription.
  • Screen Capture / MediaProjection (Required for screen recording): Records your screen locally when you start a session.
  • Display over other apps / Overlay (Optional): Shows a floating control bubble so you can manage your recording while using other apps.
  • Notifications (Optional): Shows recording status, progress, and controls in your notification bar.
  • Internet (Required): Sends audio to Groq for transcription and verifies subscription status with RevenueCat / Google Play.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Groq API (Whisper transcription): Audio tracks are sent to Groq's servers for speech-to-text transcription only. Audio is not retained by Groq after transcription is complete.
  • RevenueCat: Handles subscription management and purchase verification via Google Play Billing. RevenueCat does not receive your audio or recordings. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: Payments are processed directly by Google Play. Scribec does not store payment information.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All recordings, transcripts, chapters, and flashcards are stored locally on your device. You can delete all data at any time via Settings → Storage → Clear all recordings inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

To request deletion of any data processed by third parties (Groq, RevenueCat), contact us at the email below.

6. Children's Privacy

Scribec is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be indicated by an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
