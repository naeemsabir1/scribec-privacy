Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Scribec ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use the Scribec Android application. We are committed to transparency and securing your information.

2. Data Collection & Processing

Scribec captures your screen and audio to provide seamless recording and transcription features. While screen recordings and video files are processed and stored entirely locally on your device, our advanced speech-to-text transcription feature requires sending audio data to a secure third-party API (Groq API). Once the audio is transcribed, the resulting text is stored locally on your device.

3. Required Permissions

To provide you with the best experience, Scribec requests the following permissions:

  • Microphone: Used to capture audio for your screen recordings and for cloud-based speech-to-text transcription.
  • Screen Capture (MediaProjection): Used solely to record your screen locally when you initiate a recording session.
  • Storage: Used to securely save your recorded video files and text transcripts directly on your device.
  • Internet: Required to securely transmit audio segments to our transcription provider (Groq) and for usage analytics.

4. Third-Party Services

The app integrates specific third-party services to deliver its core features and maintain reliability. Audio tracks are processed by Groq API for Whisper transcription. Additionally, the app may use services for analytics, crash reporting, and monetization (e.g., Google Play Billing for subscriptions). These services adhere to their respective privacy policies.
