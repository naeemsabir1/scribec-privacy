Privacy Policy for Scribec

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Scribec ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use the Scribec Android application.

2. Data Collection & On-Device Processing

Scribec is designed with privacy in mind. The core features of the app, including screen recording, microphone capture, and speech-to-text transcription, operate entirely locally on your device. We do not transmit your screen recordings, audio files, or generated transcripts to any external servers.

3. Required Permissions

  • Microphone: Used solely to capture audio for your screen recordings and to generate local transcripts.
  • Screen Capture (MediaProjection): Used solely to record your screen when you initiate a recording session.
  • Storage: Used to save your recorded video files and transcripts locally on your device.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services that collect information used to identify you, specifically for analytics, crash reporting, and monetization (Google Play Billing for subscriptions). These services have their own privacy policies.
